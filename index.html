The lights are your walls

Husks only exist where you can't see them. Your turrets can only shoot what your lamps light up. But every lamp is a beacon, and every drop of Bloom you mine makes the next night stranger.

Build within cable reach of a powered lamp. Harvesters go on glowing Bloom pools.

Light is territory

  • Lamps chain power outward from the Core. Anything you build must sit within cable reach of a powered lamp.
  • Turrets only fire at husks standing in light.
  • Each lit source adds to Glow. Waves grow with Glow, so a bigger base draws a bigger crowd.

Bloom is a bargain

  • Harvesters pull Bloom from the pools you can see glowing in the dark.
  • Every unit mined raises Mutation. Higher mutation means tougher husks and new strains.
  • Spend 50 Bloom on a Purge to knock mutation back down.

Know the strains

  • Walker: slow and steady.
  • Runner: fast and fragile. Common as mutation rises.
  • Bloater (15%+): bursts when it dies or touches a building and snuffs nearby lamps for 10 seconds. Cut cables go dark downstream. Kill it far out.
  • Shade (45%+): hunts harvesters and shrugs off half of turret fire.
