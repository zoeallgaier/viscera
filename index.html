The lights are your walls

Drones hide in the dark. Your turrets only hit what your servers light up, but every server is also a beacon.

  • Light is territory. Servers chain power out from the Base. Build within cable reach of a lit server.
  • Light is a beacon. More servers mean more Data, and bigger waves.
  • Lithium is your budget. Server 1 · Refinery 2 · Turret 3 · Missiles 5. Refineries on glowing pools earn 1 Lithium every 8 seconds, and each wave you survive pays 2. Pools run dry, so push into the dark for more.
  • Turrets vs. missiles. Turrets fire fast at close range. Missile launchers reach farther and hit groups with splash damage, but reload slowly.
  • Waves get worse. Panzers (wave 3+) burst and snuff nearby servers for 10s, cutting power downstream. Infiltrators (wave 6+) hunt refineries.

Drag a piece from the pad onto the board, or tap a tile to build. Long-press a building to dismantle it.
