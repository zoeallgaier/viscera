The lights are your walls

Husks hide in the dark. Your turrets only hit what your lamps light up, but every lamp is also a beacon.

  • Light is territory. Lamps chain power out from the Core. Build within cable reach of a lit lamp.
  • Light is a beacon. More lamps mean more Glow, and bigger waves each night.
  • Bloom is your budget. Harvesters on glowing pools earn it. Pools run dry, so you'll have to push into the dark for more.
  • Nights get worse. Bloaters (night 3+) burst and snuff nearby lamps for 10s, cutting power downstream. Shades (night 6+) hunt harvesters.

Tap a tile to build. Long-press a building to dismantle it.
